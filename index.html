Day 1 · New connection

First message

Hi {{First Name}}, Congratulations on your new business! I know how much work goes into building something from the ground up. I work with local business owners to help protect both their business and their personal income if an unexpected illness or injury prevents them from working. Many owners don't realize that even a temporary disability can create major financial stress for both the business and their family. I'd love to learn more about your business and see if there are any simple, affordable ways to help protect what you're building. There's absolutely no obligation—just a short conversation to see if it makes sense. Would you have 15 minutes sometime this week or next for a quick call?
Day 3 · Follow-up

Key question

"If you were unable to work for six months, would your business continue to pay its bills?" Offer a complimentary Business Protection Review, no pressure.

Discovery

Questions to ask

  • Tell me about your business / how long have you been running it.
  • Who handles things if you're away?
  • What would happen if you couldn't work for 3–6 months? What expenses continue?
  • Do you have any disability or overhead expense coverage today?
  • What are your biggest financial concerns if you're unable to work?

Spend most of the meeting listening — their answers reveal the opportunity.

Closing

Transition & close

"Based on what you've shared, I'd like to show you a few options that may help protect both your personal income and your business if the unexpected happens." "If we can put a plan in place that fits your budget and addresses the concerns you mentioned, would you be comfortable moving forward today?"

If they hesitate: "What questions or concerns would you like to address before making a decision?"

Referral

Ask, even if they don't buy

"I appreciate your time today. I enjoy helping local business owners prepare for the unexpected. Is there another business owner you know who might benefit from the same complimentary review?"
